Incudo is a character builder for tabletop role-playing games. It is free, open source under the MIT licence, and has no accounts, no ads and no paywall.

Build player characters and NPCs step by step, then read and play the finished sheet on your phone.

Any game system
The app has no built-in knowledge of one game. A game is described by a system definition, a data file. It comes with one for the fifth edition rules of the System Reference Document 5.2.1. You can copy it and change it, or write your own.

Reads Aurora content
Incudo reads content written for Aurora Builder, including the community content maintained by AuroraLegacy, and imports Aurora saved characters. Incudo is not made by, or affiliated with, the makers of Aurora Builder.

Works offline
Once a content source is downloaded, the app builds characters with no connection. Your characters stay on your phone.

No rulebook content
Incudo ships no rulebook text. What you build with comes from the content sources you add, written and maintained by people. Incudo reads that content and does not change it.

Open source
The code is on GitHub at github.com/WCH-Softworks/Incudo, where you can read it, report a problem or suggest a change.
